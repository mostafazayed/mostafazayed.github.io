Noorania — Terms of Use

Last updated: 26 August 2026

These terms cover your use of the Noorania app. Using the app means accepting them. Apple's Standard End User License Agreement also applies to the app itself.

What the app is

Noorania teaches Arabic reading by the Noorania Qaida method: recorded lessons to listen to, and pronunciation practice that scores what you say. The pronunciation score is an aid to learning, not a religious ruling or a certification, and it should not be treated as a substitute for a qualified teacher.

Free use

Every lesson and its audio can be used without paying and without an account. Free use includes advertising and a daily allowance of pronunciation scoring.

Subscriptions

  • Noorania offers a monthly and a yearly subscription. Each removes advertising and raises the pronunciation-scoring allowance.
  • The price and the period are shown in the app before you buy, in your own currency, and payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation.
  • A subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Your Apple ID is charged for renewal within 24 hours of the period ending.
  • You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period runs to its end.

Practice packs

A practice pack is a one-off purchase that adds a fixed amount of pronunciation scoring time. It is consumed as you use it and does not renew.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are Apple's to give. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot refund an App Store purchase ourselves.

Restoring purchases

Purchases are tied to your Apple ID. On a new device, use Restore purchases on the subscription screen.

Acceptable use

The lesson recordings and the printed pages in the app are licensed for personal learning. Please do not redistribute them, extract them for another product, or try to reach the scoring service other than through the app.

Availability

The app is provided as it is. Lessons work offline once installed; sign-in, progress sync and pronunciation scoring need a connection and can be interrupted by things outside our control.

Changes

If these terms change materially, the date at the top changes with them.
